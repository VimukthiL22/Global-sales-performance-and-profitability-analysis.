# Global Sales Performance Dashboard 📊

An interactive end-to-end business intelligence project that analyzes global sales performance, profitability, and customer segments. The project involves data preprocessing using **Python (Pandas)** and interactive dashboard development in **Power BI**.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable executive insights. It tracks key retail metrics like Total Sales, Total Profit, and Average Order Value (AOV) across different regions, product categories, and payment methods to help business leaders make data-driven decisions.

### Key Insights Addressed:
* **Profitability Leakage:** Identifies which regions and customer segments drive the highest margins.
* **Product Performance:** Ranks top-performing products by sales volume.
* **Payment Optimization:** Tracks how transaction types (Credit Card, PayPal, etc.) impact net profitability.
* **Seasonality & Trends:** Monitors monthly and yearly performance fluctuations across 2024 and 2025.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Workflow

### 1. Data Sourcing & Preprocessing (Python)
* **Source:** Global Sales Dataset sourced from Kaggle.
* **Libraries:** `pandas`, `numpy`
* **Operations Performed:**
  * Cleaned missing values and handled data anomalies.
  * Structured and standardized data types (dates, currency, categories).
  * Optimized the dataset to ensure high performance and fast loading times inside Power BI.

### 2. Business Intelligence & Visualization (Power BI)
* Built a dynamic data model with robust relationships.
* Designed an intuitive, dark-themed UI/UX layout optimized for executive scannability.
* Leveraged advanced Power BI visual elements to enable deep-dive analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Breakdown

### 1. Executive KPIs
Located at the top of the report, these cards display instant health metrics for the business:
* **Total Sales:** \$450.50K
* **Total Profit:** \$147.72K
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** \$242.10

### 2. Core Visuals & Charts
* **Geographic Sales Distribution (Map):** A spatial breakdown of sales volume across North America, South America, Europe, Asia, and Australia.
* **Profit Decomposition Tree:** A dynamic, hierarchical visual allowing users to drill down into total profit by **Region** and further isolate performance by **Customer Segment** (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office).
* **Top 10 Products by Sales (Bar Chart):** Highlights top revenue-generating inventory, heavily led by ergonomic office furniture and standing desks.
* **Profitability by Payment Method (Waterfall Chart):** Illustrates the cumulative buildup of profit across payment streams, identifying Credit Card and PayPal as core drivers.
* **Sales & Profit Trends (Dual-Axis Line Chart):** Plots monthly revenue alongside profit margins over a two-year timeline (2024–2025) to catch seasonal trends.

---
📈 Key Insights
Top Performers: Office furniture (Ergonomic Chairs and Standing Desks) are the primary revenue drivers.

Profitability: North America and Europe are the most profitable regions, specifically within the Consumer segment.

Payment Efficiency: Digital payments (Credit Card and PayPal) account for the largest share of settled profit
